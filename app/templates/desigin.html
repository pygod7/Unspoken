Our Core Features

MoodLift leverages cutting-edge technology to offer impactful mental health support. Our core features are accessible, anonymous, and deeply insightful.

Anonymous Chat & Journaling

Express yourself freely through secure, anonymous chat or private journaling. AI analyzes entries to detect early stress signals.

AI-Powered Insights

Personalized tips, exercises, and coping strategies tailored to your needs. Urgent flags for professional help when needed.

Mood Tracking & Analytics

Record daily moods and visualize trends with intuitive graphs for greater self-awareness.

Peer & Community Support

Connect anonymously with peers in moderated groups facing similar challenges.

Guided Meditation & Yoga

Mindfulness exercises, breathing techniques, and yoga sessions to foster calm.

Professional Help

Direct access to counselors, psychologists, and crisis resources.
