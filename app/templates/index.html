FAQ

Quick answers about privacy, safety, and how MoodLift works.

Is MoodLift anonymous? +

Yes. We support students without exposing identity. Our goal is to remove stigma and make help feel safe.

Does MoodLift diagnose mental illness? +

No. We provide well-being signals and support pathways, not medical diagnoses. For severe cases, we guide you to professionals.

How do peer groups stay safe? +

Groups are structured, time-bound, and AI-moderated to encourage healing and prevent harmful behavior.

What happens in crisis situations? +

We use human-reviewed escalation and connect you to crisis resources, hotlines, and counselors when risk is high.

Can universities see student messages? +

No. Institutions only get anonymized insights like well-being heatmaps. Never personal journals or conversations.

Who is MoodLift built for? +

Students first, with partnerships for universities, NGOs, and CSR programs to scale globally.
